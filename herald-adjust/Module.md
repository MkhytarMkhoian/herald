# Module herald-adjust

The Adjust adapter, over an `AdjustInstance` and an `AdjustConfig` the app builds. Events reach
Adjust through dashboard tokens. For revenue, the app maps its events to `AdjustRevenueEvent` and
`AdjustAdRevenueEvent`. A fresh install stays silent until consent.

See the [Adjust page](https://mkhytarmkhoian.github.io/herald/vendors/adjust/).
